THE ANCIENT ISLAND

A civilization shaped by vision.

Ancient Sri Lankan civilization was shaped by remarkable achievements in architecture, agriculture, engineering, art and spirituality. Generations of people transformed the island's landscape into a network of magnificent cities, reservoirs, monasteries and monuments.

From the royal capitals of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa to the extraordinary fortress of Sigiriya, these places continue to tell the story of a civilization whose achievements remain visible today.
